# D11 Batch 1 candidate review

This batch reviewed 24 already-generated candidate pairs across all seven
materialized DEV source groups. No PDFs were reopened, no pages were rendered,
and candidate mining was not rerun. Semantic labels derive only from the stored
local crop, broader context crop, and fragment/gap overlay.

The batch contains 12 `STRUCTURAL_CONTINUATION`, 8
`ANNOTATION_OR_GLYPH`, 1 `AMBIGUOUS`, and 3 `INVALID_FOR_TASK` cases.
Hard negatives cover aligned text glyphs, a dimension chain, drawing-layout,
legend-table, revision-table, and title-block rules.

No selected cases share a repeated-line or repeated-dimension-chain cluster;
therefore zero selected near-duplicates were removed. More review is needed
before the overall D11 feasibility decision because this batch alone does not
establish the distribution of the remaining 68 representative proposals.

Production semantic delta: none. Validation and locked-blind data were not
accessed.
